# Analytical Reports, SQL Deliverables & Executive Summaries

This directory contains analytical findings reports, visual artifacts, SQL deliverables, and executive summaries for the Telco Customer Churn & Retention Intelligence project.

---

## Catalog of Reports & Assets

### 1. [reports/sql_analysis.md](sql_analysis.md)
The comprehensive technical and business report for Phase 3 (SQL Analytics & Business Query Engineering):
- Relational schema design and justification for SQLite.
- Data loading methodology and data hygiene audits.
- Executive business intelligence scorecard and KPI definitions.
- Detailed churn driver findings (contract, internet, payment channels, add-on services).
- Two-dimensional customer risk segmentation matrix ($3 \times 3$ Tenure $\times$ Spend) and Pareto concentration analysis.
- Advanced SQL engineering demonstrations (CTEs, Window Functions, running totals, `PARTITION BY`).
- Independent SQL vs. Pandas reconciliation audit results ($37 / 37$ checks passed).
- Methodological discussion: Snapshot MRR exposure proxy vs. realized revenue loss, and cross-sectional limitations.

### 2. [reports/eda_findings.md](eda_findings.md)
The primary analytical report synthesizing Phase 2 findings across all 4 Jupyter notebooks:
- Executive summary of customer churn and revenue exposure.
- Verified findings across contract, tenure, payment, internet, and value-added service domains.
- Inferential hypothesis testing, Cramér's V ranking, Mann-Whitney U, and Cohen's d effect sizes.
- Answers to 8 core business stakeholder questions.

### 3. [reports/excel/](excel/)
Deliverables for Phase 4 (Excel Business Analysis & Interactive Dashboard):
- [`customer_churn_analysis.xlsx`](excel/customer_churn_analysis.xlsx): Production 5-sheet financial and customer retention model featuring Excel Table `CustomerData`, dynamic formulas (`COUNTIF`, `SUMIF`, `AVERAGEIF`, `XLOOKUP`), multi-dimensional cross-tabulations, and an executive KPI dashboard with 4 native charts.
- [`README.md`](excel/README.md): Exhaustive workbook guide, worksheet architectures, formula index, and technical disclosures.
- [`excel_validation.md`](excel/excel_validation.md): Quantitative benchmark reconciliation audit proving exact reconciliation across all portfolio KPIs (41/41 checks passed).

### 4. [reports/sql_results/](sql_results/)
Exported analytical datasets generated by [src/run_sql_analytics.py](../src/run_sql_analytics.py):
- `business_kpis.csv`: Executive scorecard metrics.
- `churn_by_contract.csv`: Churn rates by contract type.
- `churn_by_internet_service.csv`: Churn rates by internet service provider.
- `churn_by_payment_method.csv`: Churn rates by payment method.
- `tenure_spend_segmentation.csv`: Full $3 \times 3$ Tenure $\times$ Spend risk matrix.
- `advanced_segment_ranking.csv`: DENSE_RANK() revenue exposure ranking.
